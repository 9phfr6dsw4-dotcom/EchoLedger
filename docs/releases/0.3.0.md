# EchoLedger 0.3.0

- Household cards can now have users: choose one person, several people, or no one in particular for each account or card in Settings. Every card still belongs to the household.
- Reports and Transactions have a new switch for the people you pick: count the spending assigned to them, or everything on the cards they use. A card several of them use is counted once in a report.
- Existing accounts keep their owner as the card's one user, and past reports do not change when you change a card's users. Use Apply to Past Transactions when you want earlier imports to follow.
- Transactions, Reports and the sheets now fit smaller windows: the transaction details panel floats over the table instead of squeezing it, the report controls scroll sideways when tight, and the sidebar scrolls in a short window. The design is unchanged.
- Bank cardholder and card-number columns are still read when a file has them, but you can ignore them completely.

## Install
Quit EchoLedger, move the old app to the Trash, then put the new EchoLedger.app in Applications. If macOS blocks the ad-hoc signed build, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Avoid uninstaller utilities; your database remains in Application Support. An optional backup is available in Settings → Data & Backups.
