# EchoLedger 0.4.0

- **Zelle in Review.** Apple Intelligence no longer files Zelle. Review asks what each one was for: spending (with a category), a gift, paying each other back and forth, income, something you sold, a gift received, someone paying you back, or don't count it. Nothing is preselected, and you can remember your answer for a name.
- **Paying each other.** One spending category for people you pay back and forth. Money out adds to it and money in lowers it, so a month shows a net amount, positive or negative. It is never income.
- **Gifts received.** Shown as its own line in Reports and counted in net cash flow, separate from income.
- **Zelle in Reports.** A new Zelle card lists everyone you Zelled with: received, sent, net, and how each is counted. Click a name to see those transactions. Transactions has a new Zelle filter.
- **Money moved within the household.** On a person's report, what was sent to or received from the others in the household is shown below the totals. It is not counted as income or spending.
- Nothing you filed before changes. Totals only change where you choose how a Zelle is filed. Your saved ledger loads exactly as before.

## Install
Quit EchoLedger, move the old app to the Trash, and put the new EchoLedger.app in Applications. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Avoid uninstaller utilities; your saved ledger remains in Application Support.

## Try it
Open a Zelle in Review and pick what it was for. Check Reports → Overview for the Zelle card, then pick one person and check "Money moved within the household". Compare your totals with 0.3.1: they should be the same until you file a Zelle differently.
