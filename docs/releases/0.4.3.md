# EchoLedger 0.4.3

- **More everyday merchant logos.** Adds over 200 curated offline marks for popular restaurants, grocery stores, beauty stores, retailers, and fuel brands, including Costco, Trader Joe's, Save Mart, In-N-Out, Habit Burger, Black Bear Diner, Panera Bread, Farmer Boys, Wienerschnitzel, Olive Garden, Wendy's, Carl's Jr., Marshalls, Ulta Beauty, Xfinity, and Mister Car Wash.
- **Broader coverage for future transactions.** The supplement includes chains beyond any current ledger, such as Meijer, Hy-Vee, Wegmans, ShopRite, Wawa, and Culver’s.
- **Common name variations.** Recognizes explicit alternate spellings and numbered-store endings while retaining neutral artwork for ambiguous names.
- **Matching app and PDF artwork.** The expanded bundled collection is shared by Transactions, merchant reports, and all three downloadable PDF styles.

Merchant matching remains entirely offline. Your saved ledger, classifications, and report figures are unchanged; no re-import is needed. Businesses absent from the collection continue to show their category symbol or initials.

## Install

Quit EchoLedger, unzip the download, and replace EchoLedger.app in Applications. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Avoid uninstaller utilities; your saved ledger remains in Application Support.
