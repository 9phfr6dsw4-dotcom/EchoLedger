# EchoLedger 0.4.4

- **Prepared company and custom artwork.** Adds 50 repaired or newly drawn icon assets to the offline collection. Company marks retain their colors and proportions; custom payment, fee, education, donation, and transfer symbols use varied colors. Prepared artwork replaces older marks for matching names.
- **Matching app and PDF icons.** Transactions, transaction details, merchant reports, and all three downloadable PDF styles use the same artwork. Generic purpose matching covers transcripts, donations, background checks, and university payments without publishing personal-name aliases.
- **Zelle logos on transfers too.** The original transaction text, bank merchant field, and saved merchant name can identify Zelle even when the displayed counterpart is a person. Zelle artwork takes priority over generic transfer icons; unrelated words such as Gazelle do not match.
- **Clearer Zelle directions.** The report shows Sent to, Received from, and Net with the bank-account perspective explained. Zelle-only transaction summaries use sent, received, and net. Net remains received minus sent.

Matching stays entirely offline. Saved transactions, classifications, income attribution, report calculations, and PDF totals are unchanged. Household transfers do not create new income. No re-import is needed.

## Install

Quit EchoLedger, unzip the download, and replace EchoLedger.app in Applications. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Your saved ledger remains in Application Support.
