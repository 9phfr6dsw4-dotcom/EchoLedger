# EchoLedger 0.4.4

- **Clearer Zelle directions.** The report table now shows Sent to, Received from, and Net. Each row identifies the person or business on the other side of the payment, with an explicit bank-account perspective and net definition.
- **Consistent Zelle transaction summaries.** Zelle-only transaction lists show sent, received, and net instead of money in and money out. Sent and received display positive amounts; net remains received minus sent.
- **Original income stays with its earner.** A fictional salary-and-household-transfer regression verifies that the sender retains the original income, the recipient gains no new income from the transfer, and household income, spending, net cash flow, and transfer volume stay intact.

This update clarifies presentation. It does not change saved transactions, payment classification, reporting calculations, or PDF totals. No re-import is needed.

## Install

Quit EchoLedger, unzip the download, and replace EchoLedger.app in Applications. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Your saved ledger remains in Application Support.
